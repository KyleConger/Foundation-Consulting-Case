# Decisions — high-school overview (briefing)

**This is an OVERVIEW.** It is for project briefings *before* the team goes into the full memo. Every number here is copied from `Decisions/decisions.md`. Do not treat this file as the source of truth. Detail, tags, funding pools, and the full if-lost-to-X playbook live in that memo.

**Audience.** About 15–16: full sentences and real terms (churn, capex, overlap), without the assumption-register density of the adult file.

**Status.** Draft work-plan, not a recommendation that has already fired. Anchor: end **Q1 2026**. **CHTR** is Charter’s ticker; **Spectrum** is the consumer brand.

---

## 1. What problem we are solving

The stock is the **symptom**. **Internet** is the **engine**.

Charter’s share price fell about **80%** over five years, then about **25%** in one day after the Q1 2026 print. The market was not mainly reacting to video, mobile, or a 2027 network date. **Root C** is the rule we use: the tape prices the **Internet print** — how many residential Internet customers Charter added or lost, and whether that slope is getting better.

In Q1 2026 that print was **−120k** Internet customers (about 2× the year-ago quarter). Internet revenue fell **1.3%**. Adj. EBITDA fell **2.2%**. Capex rose **+19%**. Free cash flow fell **−12%**. The company is not in financial distress. Owners sold the *direction*: spend went up, the profit engine shrank, and the programs meant to offset it (rural, network upgrades, video/mobile optics) did not change the Internet slope.

Our job is not “explain why the stock is down” as a story. It is to name the **capability gaps** Charter has to close so operations and marketing can act — and to keep every later play **dormant** until they can see *where* they are losing.

## 1a. Three types of decisions going forward

Not a new diagnosis. Same GAP. Same numbers. From here the work breaks into three types of decisions. Each type has a folder.

**1. Churn (if-lost-to-X then Y)** — folder `Decisions/CHURN/`. If they lose a customer to move-out, **FWA**, **fiber**, or **nonpay**, then do the matching play. First they need **G1**: a quarterly **net-loss** tracker by category. Cells are empty on purpose. The **54k excess** versus Comcast (**1.8×**) is unallocated until the first fill. **−66k** is a *ceiling* after that fill, not the briefing plan.

**2. EH&B expansion — stop the Internet bleed** — folder `Decisions/EH&B/`. **EH&B** means existing homes and businesses: grow **Internet** on plant already built (the **HFC**/fiber-powered network already passed), not new rural miles. Target is **net adds** (**Root C**: the tape prices the Internet print). **Overlap GTM** versus **FWA**/fiber. **Cox** is not the closer. Rural leftover miles cannot close a **179k** quarterly peer gap.

**3. Better utilize previous capex** — folder `Decisions/IMP TO CAPEX/`. Get more Internet out of capital already spent. Score **capex** dollars per net Internet add. Freeze “wait for 2027 / run the upgrade harder” (rebuild already **+71%**). Raise rural **take** on lit plant (**38% → 54%**) rather than more miles.

Sequence still cheapest binary first: tracker yes/no before firing **churn** plays. The six recommendation areas map **1–2 → CHURN**, **5–6 → EH&B**, **3–4 → IMP TO CAPEX**.

## 2. Why the stock fell (three causes, short)

Not one narrative. Three causes at once.

**Poor capex.** Charter spent **$11.7B** in FY2025 — **21.3% of revenue**, about **2×** Comcast’s cable-like Connectivity & Platforms segment (**10.8%** of that segment’s revenue). **Capex intensity** is high. In Q1, spend rose and Internet still went negative. The market is punishing plant that does not produce Internet customers. Waiting for network-evolution completion at **end-2027** is management’s existing capital story. It does not, by itself, answer the CEO charge.

**Failed rural.** The rural program is converting — **+41k** customer relationships in Q1 — and it still cannot close the hole. Core (company minus rural) lost **−204k**. Rural covers about **20%** of that core hole. Take on already-lit rural plant is stuck near **38%** versus a company **54%**. Rural is **1.6%** of company revenue. Leftover miles to the 1.7 million-passing floor are a one-time stock, smaller than one quarter of the Comcast slope gap. **RDOF/BEAD** (federal rural-broadband subsidies) make pause or exit costly: miss the rules and the FCC can treat Charter as in default — penalties, forfeitures, withheld support. Do not add rural miles to fix a core-product gap. Do not assume an exit the subsidy contracts do not allow.

**Market trends.** People move less, so the cheap truck-roll add at a new address shrank. **FWA** (fixed wireless access — a self-install box from a wireless carrier) made leaving cheaper. Winning a **non-mover** got more expensive because Charter has to *displace* an incumbent, not greet a mover. Video used to raise the cost of leaving Internet; cord-cutting removed that glue. Those industry shifts are the floor. They do **not** by themselves explain why Charter’s residential Internet net-loss rate is **1.8× Comcast** in Q1, an **54k excess**. Industry weather plus a worse firm slope is the full story.

## 3. The internal gap

Ops and marketing share an insight gap. Filings do not prove which department owns the data.

**What they can see.** Company-level **net adds**: **−120k** Internet in Q1. Rural **+41k** is a disclosed program add, not a residential-Internet loss tracker. The existing network-churn work used a **net-loss rate** versus Comcast (**1.8×**; excess **54k**) as a company-level proxy. **Gross churn** is unused because it is not disclosed. We will not invent a filled-in breakdown they do not have.

**What they cannot see.** (a) The true **churn** *rate* — gross disconnects divided by the starting base — versus failed acquisition (gross adds collapsing). Net **−120k** is the leftover of both. (b) *Where* they are losing: **core vs rural**; **overlap** versus the rest of the footprint (**AT&T ~27% / Verizon ~16%** of plant with terrestrial 100 Mbps+); **move-out** versus competitive (**FWA** vs **fiber**) versus involuntary/**nonpay**; tenure; product mix at leave.

Until they can see rate and place, every later recommendation (cut rural, retarget capex, reprice versus FWA, change the overlap offer) is a guess.

**G1** is the gate. It is a standing **quarterly net-loss-by-category** tracker for residential Internet. Not a one-off churn file. Not call-center reason codes (agents have favorite codes — sample the disconnect). Working assumption, to confirm with the CEO: past customer data has not been tracked in a usable form.

The unit is quarterly **net** loss, or gross adds versus disconnects if they can get them. Cells stay **empty on purpose** until the system exists.

Ask the CEO one yes/no question first: *Will you stand up — or do you already have — a quarterly net-loss-by-category tracker for residential Internet on the core?* If yes, skip the build and run mix control. If no, standing up G1 **is** the next step. Capex productivity and rural take become decision-sized only after the first fill.

## 5. If-lost-to-X (dormant until first fill)

We **can** name a response for each leak once the tracker exists. We **will not** fire any of those responses until the cells are filled. This is a map, not a mix we have measured. Company-wide upgrade/rebuild (already **+71%** in Q1) and more rural miles do **not** fire as the default answer to any leak.

A branch fires only if it is the largest — or a co-equal — cell in the first fill **and** closing Charter’s gap versus Comcast in that cell would absorb a material share of the **54k excess**. Co-equal cells: fire the cheaper lever first. Two expensive levers do not fire in the same quarter.

| If lost to X | Then do Y (once the cell is the leak) |
|---|---|
| **Move-out** | Failed acquisition / housing, not a save-offer problem. Same-day transfer-of-service and MDU/landlord capture. Do not wait for housing to recover. Do not add rural miles as a mover substitute. |
| **Competitive FWA** | Easy-out (self-install, price-sensitive). Internet rate-lock / FWA-match save plus self-install WiFi, on **and** off overlap. Do not treat FWA as a network-rebuild problem. On-overlap FWA may be a stepping-stone to fiber — then hand to the fiber play. |
| **Competitive fiber** | Overlap-zip GTM, not a company rebuild. Gig/symmetry save targeting AT&T ~27% and Verizon ~16% zips. Node-split **only in those zips and only if the sample names speed**. Do not raise company capex intensity. Do not wait for end-2027. |
| **Involuntary / nonpay** | Credit and collections, not GTM and not plant. Payment arrangement before disconnect; origination screen; 90-day first-bill save. Field + care opex already fell **$30M** in Q1 — if nonpay is the leak, that “savings” was the wrong cut. |

**WHERE overlay.** If **core** is the Internet leak, fire the reason play on the core — do not add rural miles (core already **−204k** vs rural **+41k** as a one-off reconstruction). If **rural** is the leak, raise take on plant already lit (**38% → 54%**), honor **RDOF/BEAD**, and stop leftover miles beyond the subsidy floor. If **overlap** is the leak versus rest of footprint, concentrate the reason play on those zips; if losses just follow the 27% / 16% plant mix, overlap is not the firm-specific story.

**WHO overlay.** Mix at leave (Internet-only / +mobile / +video) and tenure size the offer. They do not pick a fifth spend program. **Root C** still holds: Spectrum Mobile net adds do not re-rate an Internet **−120k**.

Spend, if any, comes from named existing pools (upgrade/rebuild increment, avoided rural remainder, restored care opex, CPE) — not from “more plant.” Full pool IDs and kill lines are in `decisions.md`.

## 6. Does it move the stock?

Closing the full **54k excess** versus Comcast would take a later Internet print to about **−66k** (range **−93k** to **−66k**). **−66k is a ceiling after G1 fill, not the briefing plan.** That is enough to **MATTER** versus the Q1 tape — most of the year-over-year slope investors sold. It is **not** enough to **RE-RATE** the five-year ~80% loss.

Half a close (**−93k**) still looks like Q1. **−59k** and zero are not playbook-claimable. The cash story in Q1 was capex, not that 54k cohort. Capex intensity ~2× Comcast and rural remainder still sit after a successful play. No price target.

**Kill on this point.** If the tracker fill shows **move-out** matching Comcast, the playbook cannot produce a Charter-specific print the tape will pay for. Then the CEO charge has to be answered with a growth model that does not wait for movers — not with more rural miles.

Q2 later public fact: Internet **−172k**. A 54k close would leave **−118k**. That does not replace the Q1 assignment anchor.

## 7. The six recommendation areas (in order)

These are jobs the files already earn, sequenced cheapest binary first. They are **not** six new ideas, and they do **not** fire empty-cell plays. Full adult write-up: `Decisions/six-recommendation-areas.md`. Each area sits in one of the three type folders as **primary**.

**1. Quarterly net-loss-by-category tracker (G1).** Type 1 · `CHURN/`. They only see **−120k**, not who left or why. **Do:** ask the CEO yes/no; if no, stand up the tracker and fill the empty cells before any reason play. **Targeting:** belief / decision quality — this unblocks the Internet-print play; it does not itself close 54k. **Funded by:** existing ops/marketing data work, not plant.

**2. One core reason-play after the first fill.** Type 1 · `CHURN/`. Fix the biggest leak only — one **if-lost-to-X** play on the **core**, not four expensive fixes, and not “build more network” as the default. **Do:** fire the single largest (or cheaper co-equal) reason branch; never two expensive levers in the same quarter. **Targeting:** **54k** → next print **−66k** (ceiling after fill, not the plan). **Funded by:** the matching named pool (P1–P4 in the full memo), not new capital. Must still move after a successful play: capex intensity and rural remainder.

**3. Capex productivity, not a 2027 date.** Type 3 · `IMP TO CAPEX/`. Score spend by dollars of capital per **net Internet add**, not miles of plant or “on time versus 2027.” **Do:** freeze company-wide evolution acceleration; redirect or cut the upgrade/rebuild increment; name what gets cut. **Targeting:** FCF and belief — Q1 capex **+19%** while Internet and FCF went the wrong way. **Funded by:** intensity already being spent (**21.3%** vs **10.8%**). No new capital.

**4. Rural take, not miles.** Type 3 · `IMP TO CAPEX/`. Sell the countryside houses already wired. **Do:** lift take on plant already lit (**38%** toward company **54%**); stop remainder beyond the subsidy floor; honor contracted **RDOF/BEAD**. **Do not** add rural miles to close the core. **Targeting:** FCF via avoided leftover spend, not the quarterly Internet slope (the arithmetic already says leftover miles cannot close the Comcast quarterly gap). **Funded by:** not spending the rural remainder.

**5. Cox offer / brand / willingness-to-pay gate.** Type 2 · `EH&B/`. **Cox** is not a magic wand. **Do:** before treating Cox as a closer, test whether those customers will take Spectrum Internet offers, brand, and price — or print Charter’s **1.8×** loss rate on a new base. Size Cox passings, customers, and debt on their own lines. **Targeting:** kill a false closer, and the first combined Internet print. **Funded by:** cheap offer tests, not a 70-million-home story.

**6. Non-mover, video-optional growth model.** Type 2 · `EH&B/`. Stop planning as if housing-driven **net adds** and video lock-in are coming back. **Do:** write a gross-add plan versus **FWA** and **fiber**, not versus last year’s mover. Same-day transfer and landlord capture is the leftover mover play, not the whole answer. Do not credit mobile against Internet (**Root C**). **Targeting:** belief — and the Internet print **if** Area 2’s kill fires (move-out matching Comcast). **Funded by:** sales-ops plus stopping retention promo on vacated addresses. Not rural miles. Not company rebuild.

## 8. Kill rule / will not recommend / unwilling to claim

**Kill rule** (written before a recommendation). If a quarterly net-loss-by-category tracker **already exists** and cuts core vs rural, overlap vs rest, reason (move-out / competitive / nonpay — sampled, not agent codes), and product mix at leave, **do not** spend the step building tracking — go to mix control.

If that tracker shows **move-out** as the single largest reason and Charter’s *competitive* loss rate matches Comcast on the core, **kill** “Charter-specific product failure” as the Q1 story.

If field + care opex stay down, losses stay up, **and** the sample does **not** name speed / WiFi / reliability as the largest reason on the core, **kill** “network performance caused the doubling.”

**We will not recommend.** Add rural miles / “execute rural harder” as the slope closer. Wait for 2027 / run the upgrade harder (upgrade/rebuild already **+71%**). Reattach video as Internet retention. Buybacks as the share-price path. Kishore’s four-musts checklist. Combined network “more than 70 million homes.” Make mobile economically meaningful as a must (steal the *refusal*, not the must). Fire a reason-specific play from empty tracker cells.

**Unwilling to claim.** Gross churn, a churn *rate*, or any filled category breakdown of where they lose. That housing alone explains Charter vs Comcast. That rural NPV is zero (low yield and scale fail, not a full DCF). That **RDOF/BEAD** can be walked away from at book value. That the 2027 date re-rates the stock without an Internet-print change (**Root C**). That mobile +368k or video losses “moderating” offset Internet **−120k**. That call-center reason codes are a breakdown. Which X is the largest leak (we have a map, not a mix). That **−66k** undoes five-year ~80%. A price target.
