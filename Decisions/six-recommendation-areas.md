## Area 1 — Quarterly net-loss-by-category tracker

**Like you're 12.** They only see the final score (−120k), not who left or why — until a quarterly board splits the losses (core vs countryside, overlap vs not, moved vs wireless vs stopped paying), they are guessing.

**Why it fits this diagnosis.** Ops and marketing share an insight gap: they see company Internet **−120k** and cannot see the churn *rate* or the *division of where* they lose, so every later recommendation would be a guess.

**Action (do / targeting / funded by).** Do: ask the CEO one binary: *Will you stand up — or do you already have — a quarterly net-loss-by-category tracker for residential Internet on the core: yes or no?* If no, stand the system up this quarter. Unit: quarterly net loss (prefer gross adds vs disconnects). Cuts: core vs rural; AT&T ~27% / Verizon ~16% overlap vs rest (those shares are **footprint mix**, not losses in those zips); reason sampled from the disconnect (move-out / FWA / fiber / nonpay — **not** agent codes); product mix at leave (Internet-only / +mobile / +video); tenure if available. Working assumption, confirm with the CEO: past customer data has not been tracked in a usable form. Targeting: belief / decision quality. Unblocks the Internet-print play. Does not itself close 54k. Funded by: existing ops/marketing data work. Not plant. Not P1 (+$280M upgrade/rebuild increment).

**Time to matter.** The question is this week. First fill in **one quarter**.

**Kill.** If the tracker already exists and is used quarterly, **do not** spend the step building tracking — go to mix control (Area 2).

**Unwilling to claim.** A filled mix; a churn *rate*; that call-center reason codes are the file; that a reason-specific play is the Q1 course of action while cells are empty.

## Area 2 — One core reason-play after the first fill

**Like you're 12.** After the board fills, fix the biggest leak only: one play, not four expensive fixes at once, and not “build more network” as the default. Following movers is not the same as laying more plant.

**Why it fits this diagnosis.** Charter’s residential Internet net-loss rate is **1.8×** Comcast’s (commit, round down from 1.87×); excess **54k** (round down from 54.4k). That is the only Charter-specific print close the playbook can claim, and it is **unallocated until the first fill**. Root C: the tape prices the Internet print; mobile **+368k / +$138M** does not offset **−120k** (Kishore’s refusal, kept as a constraint).

**Action (do / targeting / funded by).** Do: after the first fill, fire the **single largest** — or cheaper co-equal — reason branch **on the core**:

| If the fill says | Do | Do not |
|---|---|---|
| Move-out | Same-day transfer-of-service + MDU/landlord capture (Community Solutions already exists), 6 months | Treat Q1 as product failure; add rural miles as a mover substitute |
| Competitive FWA | 12-month Internet rate-lock / FWA-match save + self-install WiFi, on **and** off overlap; test 2 quarters | Upgrade/rebuild (already +71%); Spectrum Mobile as the tape offset. On-overlap FWA may be a fiber stepping-stone → hand to fiber |
| Competitive fiber | Overlap-zip gig/symmetry save (AT&T ~27% / Verizon ~16% zips), Internet-only first, 2–4 quarters. Node-split **only in those zips and only if the sample names speed** | Company-wide evolution; union of 27%+16% as a 43% program |
| Involuntary / nonpay | Payment arrangement before disconnect, origination screen, 90-day first-bill save, 2 quarters | Competitive reprice; more plant |

Do **not** fire two of {FWA price match, overlap rebuild, collections restore, mover MDU} in the same quarter. Cheapest binary first. Company-wide upgrade/rebuild does **not** fire as the default to any leak except fiber **and** a speed-named sample on overlap.

Targeting: Internet print **54k → −66k** (range **−93k to −66k**). 88% of the 61k YoY slope. **Must still move after a successful play:** capex intensity ~2× Comcast C&P, rural remainder, FCF. Half a close (**−93k**) still looks like Q1. **−59k** and zero are not playbook-claimable.

Funded by: the matching named pool — not new capital:

| ID | Pool | Figure | May fund |
|---|---|---:|---|
| P1 | Q1 upgrade/rebuild increment | $675M vs $395M = **+$280M** | Overlap node/speed **if** fiber + sample names speed |
| P2 | Rural remainder to 1.7M floor | 315k × ~$5,900 ≈ **$1.9B** avoided | Avoided spend if the leak is core |
| P3 | Field + customer-ops opex YoY | **−$30M** | Restore collections/care **if** nonpay |
| P4 | Q1 CPE | **+41%** | Cut if new-tenure quality is the leak |

**Time to matter.** 2–4 quarters after first fill (mover capture: 6 months).

**Kill.** If the fill shows **move-out matching Comcast**, closable excess → **0**. The tape will not pay for a Charter-specific play. Hand the CEO charge to Area 6. If a later quarter still prints on the Q1 side of **−89.5k** after the test window, kill “the playbook close is large enough to notice.”

**Unwilling to claim.** Which X is the largest leak until fill. A price target or a reverse of ~25% / ~80%. That **−66k** re-rates five-year ~80%. That mobile holds leavers. That $43M is the FCF story. That Builds 1 and 2 agreeing on 54k validates closability — they share the peer rate.

## Area 3 — Capex productivity (spend mix, not a 2027 date)

**Like you're 12.** They spend about twice as much plant money as Comcast’s cable side, and extra upgrade dollars did not stop internet losses — score spend by whether it keeps or wins internet customers.

**Why it fits this diagnosis.** FY2025 Charter spent **$11.7B** — **21.3% of revenue**, **51% of Adj. EBITDA** — vs Comcast Connectivity & Platforms **10.8% / 27%**. Q1 capex **+19%** ($2.86B vs $2.40B), upgrade/rebuild **+71%**, Internet **−120k**, FCF **−12.3%**. The market is punishing plant that does not produce Internet customers. Waiting for end-2027 is management’s existing story; O’Donnell already flagged “run the upgrade harder” as the move most likely to fail the out-of-the-box test.

**Action (do / targeting / funded by).** Do: reclassify FY capex by **$ of capex per net Internet add**, by bucket (evolution / rural / CPE / other). A 2027 completion date is not a productivity metric. **Freeze company-wide evolution acceleration.** Redirect a slice of **P1 (+$280M)** onto overlap plant **only if** Area 2 fires the fiber branch with a speed-named sample; otherwise **cut** non-overlap rebuild. Name what gets cut: rural remainder (Area 4 / P2), non-overlap evolution, CPE if new-tenure quality is the leak (P4). Do **not** raise company capex intensity. Do **not** wait for 2027 roll-off as the course of action. Targeting: FCF and belief. The cash print was **+$456M** capex vs a **$192M** FCF hole. Area 2’s cohort cannot substitute. Funded by: intensity already being spent (21¢ of every revenue dollar vs peer 11¢). No new capital.

**Time to matter.** Next two budget cycles. FCF can print in **1–2 quarters** if spend is actually cut.

**Kill.** Mix-adjusted core Internet losses converge with Comcast **while intensity stays high** — then the spend *is* buying the print, just with lag.

**Unwilling to claim.** That 2027 completion re-rates the stock without an Internet-print change (Root C). That a 54k close heals FCF. That more upgrade/rebuild is the Q1 slope closer (already +71%; Invincible WiFi had one month of the print).

## Area 4 — Rural take on plant already lit (not more miles)

**Like you're 12.** Sell the countryside houses already wired (38% take vs ~54% company): cheaper than more miles. Leftover build cannot plug the old-neighborhood hole, and subsidies make walking away expensive.

**Why it fits this diagnosis.** Rural is doing what it was built to do (**+41k CR**, 38% take stuck 8 quarters) and still cannot close the hole (covers **20%** of a **−204k** core CR identity; **1.6%** of company revenue). Remainder to the 1.7M floor is **315k** passings → **120k** CR at 38% take, **170k** at company 54% — a **one-time stock** smaller than one quarter of the **179k** Comcast slope gap. RDOF/BEAD: miss the rules and the FCC can treat Charter as in default (penalties, forfeitures, withheld support). Sunk plant plus a ten-year subsidy obligation is not a program you pause because the core is bleeding.

**Action (do / targeting / funded by).** Do: harvest take on **1.385M already-lit** passings (**38% → 54% = 221k “missing” CR**) over **4–8 quarters**. Stop remainder passings **beyond the subsidy floor** if take stays stuck (**P2 ~$1.9B** avoided). Honor contracted RDOF/BEAD miles. **Do not add rural miles to close the core.** Targeting: FCF via avoided remainder. Not the quarterly Internet slope — the arithmetic already says it cannot close 179k/quarter. 221k is a stock on lit plant, and rural CR is **not** an Internet cut. Funded by: not spending P2. No new capital.

**Time to matter.** Remainder stop is a **this-year** budget decision. Take lift **4–8 quarters**.

**Kill.** “Rural as closer” of the Comcast *quarterly* slope — remainder + take cannot. If rural *Internet* (once disclosed) is converting and core is the hole, do not fire this as the slope play — **still do not add miles**.

**Unwilling to claim.** Rural NPV is zero (low yield and scale fail, not a DCF). RDOF/BEAD can be walked away from at book value. 221k take lift is a quarterly run-rate. Rural +41k CR is Internet conversion.

## Area 6 — Non-mover, video-optional growth model

**Like you're 12.** Stop planning like 2015: people move less, they do not need cable TV to keep internet, and you have to win people who already have a provider — versus wireless and fiber, not versus last year’s mover.

**Why it fits this diagnosis.** Three structural shifts hit at once (Root A): reduced moving (10-K: new customers depend on occupied-housing growth; the cheap add was a truck at a new address), FWA made leaving cheap while winning a non-mover got expensive, and video lock-in is gone (video revenue **−9.4%** FY25, **−9.2%** Q1, **−60k** customers). Those do **not** by themselves explain 1.8× Comcast. If Area 2’s kill fires (move-out matching the peer), **this area is the CEO-charge answer**.

**Action (do / targeting / funded by).** Do: write a gross-add plan that does **not** assume (a) housing-driven gross adds return or (b) video still locks the connectivity relationship. Price/promo and overlap GTM vs **FWA and fiber**, not vs last year’s mover. Same-day transfer-of-service and MDU/landlord capture is the **residual** mover play (funded by sales-ops and by stopping retention promo on vacated addresses) — not the whole answer, and not a wait for turnover to recover. **Retire video-reattach** and programmer-app bundles as Internet retention. Do **not** credit mobile +368k / +$138M against Internet (Root C). Targeting: belief. Internet print **if** the tracker shows move-out matching Comcast (then Area 2 cannot produce a Charter-specific print the tape will pay for). Funded by: sales-ops + stopping retention promo on vacated addresses. Not rural miles. Not P1 rebuild. Not video promo.

**Time to matter.** 6–12 months to a plan the tape can believe. TOS/MDU: 6 months if R1 fires.

**Kill.** Housing turnover **and** video attach recover **and** Charter’s loss rate converges with Comcast without offer change.

**Unwilling to claim.** Video “losses moderating” (−60k vs Comcast −322k) is connectivity relief. Mobile re-rates Internet. Buybacks as the share-price path (check-in #2: buybacks are not holding the price; cash is a constraint on funding, not the April tape). That housing alone explains Charter vs Comcast.

## Kill rules (written before the six, still binding)
