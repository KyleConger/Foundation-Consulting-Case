5  ·  THE PLAYBOOK — SCENARIOS 1 & 2 | If it's fixed wireless, price attacks; if it's fiber, the network answers first | SCENARIO 1  —  FWA-DRIVEN LOSSES | SIGNAL   | >35–40% of losses map to FWA-covered addresses; skew low-tenure / low-ARPU; audited exit data cites price. | PLAY   | Convergence pricing attack: internet + unlimited mobile at/below T-Mobile Home Internet's price point, offered only in FWA-exposed zones; market on speed and capacity superiority. | ECONOMICS GATE   | Bundle discount must be < churn-avoided LTV. Requires MVNO wholesale cost per line (data-pack ask). If the gate fails, the play “works” while destroying value — do not run it. | PROOF   | Churn delta, treated vs untreated FWA zones, within 2 quarters — run as a pre-registered controlled experiment. | KILL   | No churn separation after 2 quarters, or bundle margin negative after CBRS-offload assumptions. | FALLBACK   | Value flanker tier/brand to defend the base without repricing it — or concede the segment and route the budget to Scenarios 2/6. | SCENARIO 2  —  FIBER-DRIVEN LOSSES | SIGNAL   | Losses skew to AT&T/Verizon/Frontier fiber DMAs; higher-ARPU customers leaving; exit data cites speed, reliability, or upload. | PLAY   | Re-sequence network evolution by competitive exposure (not construction cost): DOCSIS 4.0 / high-split first where fiber overlaps; pair completed zones with symmetric-speed win-back campaigns. | ECONOMICS GATE   | Re-sequencing premium vs LTV at risk in exposed DMAs; the end-2027 completion target is the binding constraint to test. | PROOF   | Charter's cleanest natural experiment: churn in evolution-complete vs incomplete overbuilt zones. | KILL   | Parity product, continued losses — completed zones bleed at the same rate.
