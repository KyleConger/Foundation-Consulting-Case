## Headline

**Enough to MATTER to investors vs the Q1 tape; not enough to RE-RATE five-year ~80%.** If the material leak is identified and the matching play works, the best the playbook can claim is a subsequent quarterly Internet print of **−66k** (range **−93k to −66k**), against the **−120k** investors sold. That is 54k of the 61k YoY slope the tape priced (**88%**, truncated). A half close (−93k) still looks like Q1. −59k (last year) and zero are not playbook-claimable. Capex intensity ~2× Comcast C&P, rural remainder, and FCF still have to move.

No price target. No buy/sell.

## Two builds, then a real cross-check

**Build 1 — print space (Frame 1).** Priced surprise = 61k YoY. Material bar = 31k close. Playbook close = 54k → print −66k, which is 23.5k past the midpoint toward last year and 7k short of −59k.

**Build 2 — peer-rate space (Frame 2).** Comcast Q1 rate 0.23% × Charter residential begin 27,641k = 62.6k expected losses; actual 117k; excess 54.4k → commit 54k. Same 54k applied to the company −120k print.

They **share** the peer rate and the 54k commit. Agreement that “54k is the close” is **correlation by construction**, not validation that the play works.

**Cross-check that shares neither the net-add identity nor $/sub:** Q1 FCF **$1,372M**. $43M of annual Internet $ is **3.1%** of that quarter’s FCF and **0.86%** of FY2025 FCF ($5,004M). A quarter of the cohort (~$11M) is **5.6%** of the **$192M** FCF hole. Capex +**$456M** is **2.4×** the FCF hole. The cash print was spend, not the 54k cohort. Root C: the tape prices the Internet **print**, which is why 54k can matter without $43M being the FCF story.

**Second cross-check, no $/sub:** 54k / 61k = 88.5% → commit **88%** of the priced slope.

Naive 25/61 × 54 = 22 ppt is an identity, **not a forecast**, and is not used as a target.

## What else still has to move (necessary, not sufficient)

Closing 54k may stop another Q1-style **Internet-print** tape. It does not unwind five-year ~80%. Still sitting after a successful play:

1. **Capex intensity ~2×** Comcast C&P (21.3% of revenue vs 10.8%). FCF/multiple can stay capped until a named spend cut or the 2027 evolution roll-off — and 2027 alone does not re-rate without an Internet-print change.
2. **Rural spend** remains expensive to leave (RDOF/BEAD) and the wrong scale for a quarterly slope closer.
3. **The tracker** must show a Charter-specific competitive leak. Unallocated 54k until the first fill.
4. **Q2 later fact −172k:** a 54k close would leave **−118k**. If that is the run-rate, 54k is not enough to look unlike Q1. Does not replace the Q1 assignment anchor.

## Kill rule (before the verdict)

If the tracker fill shows the leak is **move-out matching Comcast** (industry housing), the playbook cannot produce a Charter-specific print the tape will pay for. Closable excess → 0. Answer the CEO charge with a growth model that does not wait for movers; do not add rural miles.

If a later quarter prints Internet net adds still on the Q1 side of −89.5k **after** a named play has had its test window (2–4 quarters), kill “the playbook close is large enough to notice.”
