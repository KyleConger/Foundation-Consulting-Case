## What’s in the markdown brief

[`CHTR-Case-Background.md`](CHTR-Case-Background.md) is a compressed extract, not a substitute for the originals. Section map:

1. **Engagement** — role, CEO (Winfrey) charge, team, dates, grading  
2. **Core tension** — stock collapse vs. a cash-generative but shrinking broadband engine  
3. **Company snapshot** — footprint, Spectrum/Xfinity brands, mix  
4. **Products**  
5. **Must-know numbers** — FY snapshot, customers, revenue mix, P&L/FCF/balance sheet, capex  
6. **Q1 2026** — the quarter that crystallized the bear case  
7. **Competition and network** — fiber, FWA, Starlink/Kuiper, COVID/gov spend, DOCSIS 4.0  
8. **Operating nuances** — restatement, labor, buybacks (and why they aren’t lifting the stock)  
9. **Pending transactions** — Cox and Liberty Broadband  
10. **Implications** — tensions the team must define (not a recommendation)  
11. **Class notes** — O’Donnell framing, evidence rules, trusted-advisor stance; *not* filing facts  
12. **Primary sources** — which filings back which facts  

**Trap:** do not mix pre- and post-Q4 2025 customer counts. Charter restated stats in Q4 2025 to include all mobile lines (including mobile-only).
