## 3. Products (as described in Item 1)

Charter states that it offers subscription-based Internet, mobile, video, and voice, priced by service mix and equipment, sold bundled or individually. Bundled services are available to substantially all passings. (`10-K FY2025`)

- **Spectrum Internet:** speeds up to 1 Gbps across the entire footprint; multi-gigabit in a portion. Company states it will offer symmetrical and multi-gigabit speeds across the entire footprint “in the next several years.” Advanced WiFi (managed WiFi, WiFi 7, Security Shield, parental controls) available to all Internet customers. Invincible WiFi described as a planned early-2026 launch (tri-band WiFi 7, 5G cellular, battery backup). `Ex99.1 Q1 2026` states Invincible WiFi launched in February (2026).
- **Spectrum Mobile:** offered to customers subscribing to Internet. Uses customer WiFi, Spectrum Mobile network (~49 million out-of-home WiFi access points plus partner WiFi), and Verizon’s cellular network. Company describes itself as an **MVNO** on Verizon. July 2025: multi-year agreement with T-Mobile to deliver mobile to Spectrum Business customers, launch set for 2026.
- **Voice:** VoIP; unlimited calling to US, Canada, Mexico, Puerto Rico; Call Guard.
- **Video:** approximately 375 channels; Spectrum TV app; cloud DVR; ~100,000 VOD titles; Xumo stream boxes deployed to new video customers. Programmer deals include ad-supported streaming apps in certain packages; Spectrum App Store launched October 2025.
- **Spectrum Business (small business):** Internet, mobile, video, voice over the fiber-powered network; Internet up to 1 Gbps.
- **Mid-market & large business:** tailored connectivity and managed services; last-mile speeds described up to 100 Gbps.
- **Spectrum Reach:** advertising (stated in course background and 10-K advertising discussion).

## 11. Competition (Charter’s Item 1 disclosure)

Internet: faces competition from FTTH, fixed wireless broadband, satellite, and DSL. Primary FTTH competitors named: AT&T and Verizon. “We face terrestrial broadband Internet (defined by the FCC as at least 100 Mbps) competition from AT&T and Verizon in approximately **27%** and **16%** of our operating footprint, respectively.” National mobile network operators offer LTE or 5G home Internet (fixed wireless access) in Charter’s markets. (`10-K FY2025`)

Mobile: competes with AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, regional operators, and other MVNOs. (`10-K FY2025`)

Video: company lists virtual MVPDs, streaming, DBS, and other sources in Item 1 (see readable 10-K Competition subsection).
